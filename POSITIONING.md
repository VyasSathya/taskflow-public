# TaskFlow Product Positioning (2026)

## The One-Liner
**"Stop switching between Todoist and Notion. TaskFlow combines the speed of Todoist with the depth of Notion—finally in one app."**

## Core Value Proposition
Capture tasks in under 200ms. Expand them into rich projects with full notes. Stay in flow.

---

## Target Customer (ICP)

### Primary: "The Overwhelmed Knowledge Worker"
- **Age**: 28-45
- **Roles**: Freelancers, consultants, project managers, content creators, developers
- **Income**: $60K-$150K
- **Current Setup**: Uses 2-3 apps (Todoist + Notion + Calendar)
- **Pain Point**: Loses 4 hours/week to app switching; ideas fall through cracks

### Job-to-be-Done
*"When I'm juggling multiple projects with tasks and notes, I want to capture everything instantly without losing context, so I can focus on execution instead of tool management."*

---

## Key Differentiators

### 1. **Hybrid Speed + Depth**
- Sub-200ms task capture (like Todoist)
- Markdown/rich text notes on every task (like Notion)
- No context switching between apps

### 2. **Features Big Players Refuse to Build**
- **Start dates + time blocking** (Todoist won't do it)
- **Fast with 10,000+ items** (Notion's Achilles heel)
- **True calendar sync** (Todoist killed it in March 2025)

### 3. **Honest Pricing**
- Free tier includes: reminders, start dates, calendar sync
- Pro: $79/year (vs Todoist $60 + Notion $96 = $156 combined)
- No paywalling basic productivity features

## Evidence-Based Pain Points

### 1. Context Switching Hell
- Workers toggle apps **1,200 times/day**
- Loses **4 hours/week** (9% of annual work time)
- 45% say app switching makes them less productive
- Source: Todoist/Asana research

### 2. Todoist Calendar Removal (March 2025)
- Legacy Google Calendar sync ended March 10, 2025
- Users forced to use Zapier (paid) or switch apps
- Reddit: "If legacy integration dies, I have no reason to stick with Todoist"
- **Opportunity**: Active switcher cohort looking for alternatives

### 3. Notion Performance Death Spiral
- Slow with >5,000 records (3-5 second loads)
- Mobile app is "sluggish" and "a chore"
- 40% of users testing alternatives due to speed
- Source: User reviews 2024-2025

### 4. Start Dates: Most Requested, Never Built
- Todoist's official stance: "Would prevent easy access to task info" (refuses to add)
- GTD practitioners forced to use tickler file workarounds
- TickTick built it—cited as #1 reason for switching
- Source: Todoist Help Center

### 5. Paywall Abuse
- Todoist: $60/year for basic reminders
- Notion: $240/year for AI (after May 2025 restructure)
- TickTick: $36/year with reminders + calendar (users cite value)
- Source: App review sites
